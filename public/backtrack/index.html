04 // ARCHITECTURAL BREAKDOWN

Under the Hood

01

Bézier Physics Loop

A custom iterative numerical solver executes cubic-bezier(0.38, 0.05, 0, 1) in a 60fps requestAnimationFrame loop to dynamically translate, resize, and morph border radius without lag.

02

Distance-Delayed Wake

Glitch particles spawn transparently and calculate Euclidean distance from the cursor (d > 28px) before fading in, leaving a clean trail that never obscures the pointer tip.

03

Magnetic Vector Pull

Hovered buttons calculate an offset delta vector from their center to the cursor, applying a gentle physical tug (5% - 8%) to the element itself.

04

Asynchronous Saccades

Creature eyes apply exponential decay with individual time constants (tau) so gaze movements follow the cursor with lifelike, natural micro-delays.
